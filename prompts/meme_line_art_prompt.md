# Meme Line Art Converter Agent & Prompt Guide

## 1. Goal
Transform a live-action photo of a person/meme (e.g. Na Moon-hee shouting) into a bold black-and-white comic line-art drawing with a rounded subtitle overlay box at the bottom.

## 2. AI Image Generation Prompts

### English Prompt (Midjourney / DALL-E / Gemini Image Gen)
> **Prompt**: Black and white vector line art style drawing of an expressive Korean elderly woman wearing glasses shouting with wide open mouth showing teeth, bold black comic book outlines, high contrast black ink on clean white background, meme webtoon style, clean lines, no gray shading, vector graphic illustration.

### Negative Prompt
> **Negative Prompt**: realistic, photo, grayscale, 3d, realistic skin texture, color, gradients, soft shading, shadows.

---

## 3. Subtitle Formatting Rules
- **Text Box**: Light gray rounded rectangle (`rgba(220, 220, 220, 0.9)`) with subtle dark outline.
- **Font**: Bold clean Korean sans-serif / comic font (e.g., NanumGothic, Maplestory, or Gothic).
- **Text Content**: `"호박 고구마!!"`
